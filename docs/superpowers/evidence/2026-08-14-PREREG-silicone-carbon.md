# PREREGISTRATION — Silicone → Carbon transmutation

**Written 2026-08-14 BEFORE the run. Predictions below are declared, not fitted.**

## The question

Can silicone-phase grammatical atoms — flexible rule substrate, apt for
fitting many situations — produce carbon-phase atoms — foundational
grammatical axioms — when they pass through the Cyclotron?

If yes, the architecture has a crystallization path: moldable chemistry
hardens into law. If no, the metaphor is quixotic: axioms are authored
(promoted in the Grimoire), not smashed into existence.

## Phase definitions (structural, not poetic)

Declared here so the classifier cannot be tuned after seeing yields.

**Silicone** — a construction is silicone when it is a flexible rule:

- ontological status is `scaffold` or `approximation`, **or**
- the bond is category-preserving (`result === left || result === right`)

These are the rules that bend. They are good at grammar-as-process.

**Carbon** — a construction is carbon when it is an axiom:

- ontological status is `grammar`, **and**
- the bond is constructive (`result` is a new type), **and**
- that result is a phrase type (`NP`, `VP`, `S`, `PP`, `INF`, `SBAR`)

These are the rules that hold a lattice. They are good at grammar-as-foundation.

**Inert** — `deprecated`, or anything that is neither.

A **transmutation** is a molecule whose every constituent atom was silicone
and whose lattice assay says the *product* is carbon. Carrying a pre-existing
carbon atom into a molecule is not transmutation. It is contamination.

## The axiom assay a carbon product must pass

A molecule of constructions is a carbon lattice only if all of:

1. every constituent is silicone (no carbon smuggled in)
2. at least one constituent is constructive
3. at least one constructive result is a phrase type
4. every constituent declares a head `0` or `1`
5. no constituent is deprecated

If (2) or (3) cannot occur in an all-silicone set — because silicone was
defined as preservative-or-scaffold, and scaffolds' new types are not phrase
types — then transmutation yield is structurally zero. That is a legal
outcome. It is the quixotic verdict.

## Arms

| Arm | Bank | What it tests |
|---|---|---|
| TREATMENT | real silicone constructions as atoms | the claim |
| PLACEBO | same left/right ports; `result` and `status` deranged | content vs topology |
| CARBON-FEED | already-carbon constructions as atoms | detector must not call identity a transmutation |
| MIXED | silicone + one carbon contaminant | F5: yield must not be the contaminant wearing a new coat |

Placebo seed, declared: **`0x53494C49`** (`SILI`). Not searched over.

## Preregistered failure modes

| # | Failure | Means |
|---|---|---|
| F1 | treatment transmutation rate ≤ placebo | no mechanism; topology alone "crystallizes" |
| F2 | CARBON-FEED transmutation rate > 0 | the detector stamps; it does not detect a phase change |
| F3 | treatment carbon products fail the axiom assay | we named them carbon without the lattice |
| F4 | treatment yield is 0 | literal transmutation is empty; axioms are authored |
| F5 | every treatment "carbon" molecule contains a carbon atom | not created, carried |

## Declared prediction

**I predict F4 fires, and therefore the claim as stated is quixotic.**

Silicone is defined as the complement of carbon's constructive-grammar-phrase
shape. A closed set of such rules cannot grow a phrase type that none of them
produces. The Cyclotron assembles; it does not author Grimoire status.

What would surprise me (and flip the verdict to legitimate):

- a silicone construction in today's Grimoire that is constructive *and*
  produces a phrase type (status scaffold/approximation, result in
  `{NP,VP,S,PP,INF,SBAR}`), **and**
- all-silicone molecules containing it pass the axiom assay, **and**
- placebo derangement of `result`/`status` drops that yield to near zero

That would mean the flexible layer already secretly contains axiom-shaped
daughters, and assembly merely reveals them. I will report that as
architecture, not poetry, if it happens.
