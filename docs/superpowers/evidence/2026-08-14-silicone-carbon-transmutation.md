## What this means for the architecture

Silicone is the right material for *writing* grammatical and semantic rules. Carbon is the right material for *founding* them. That distinction is real — 72 vs 11 in today's Grimoire, and the classifier is structural.

The leap that does not hold: putting silicone in the Cyclotron does not produce carbon as a *new* product better than scrambling the same ports. The machine assembles. It does not author `status: grammar`. Promotion remains a human Grimoire act.

The useful residue is the surprise list. Those thirteen approximations are the actual candidates for carbon promotion — not because the Cyclotron transmuted them, but because they already have axiom geometry and only lack grammar status. That is a graduation queue, not a nuclear reaction.
