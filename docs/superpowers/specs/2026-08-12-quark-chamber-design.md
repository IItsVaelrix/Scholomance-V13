# Design: The Quark Chamber — Endogenous Bond Vocabulary for the Semantic Valence Cyclotron

**Date:** 2026-08-12
**Status:** Design approved, spec under review. No implementation started.
**Contract (proposed):** `PB-QUARK-CHAMBER-v1`
**Depends on:** `2026-08-09-pressure-field-governor-pdr.md` (Phase 0 shipped/audited), `codex/core/semantic-calculus/permission.ts`, `codex/core/pixelbrain/codebase-nuclei-bank.js`

## 1. The problem, measured

The Semantic Valence Cyclotron cannot produce novel structure because its bond
vocabulary is closed and its energy landscape is flat. Both are measured, not
asserted.

### 1.1 The licensed graph is closed

`semantic-valence-cyclotron.js:connectionBetween` admits an edge iff
`offer === seek` or a hand-authored bridge rule matches. On the ritual bank:

```
atoms                 44
port-edges            98   (86 exact, 12 from 8 authored bridge rules)
connected atom-pairs  94 / 946  =  9.9%
```

The remaining 90.1% of atom pairs are not weakly bonded — they are
**unrepresentable**. No search pressure reaches them, because search samples the
graph and does not extend it.

### 1.2 Search pressure has already been tried and does not move the ceiling

`docs/superpowers/evidence/2026-08-11-cyclotron-occupancy-entropy.json`, control
vs. entropy-dampener treatment, 100k trials:

| | control | treatment |
|---|---|---|
| uniqueMolecules | 25,167 | 46,471 (+85%) |
| meanNovelty | 0.319079 | 0.324230 (+0.005) |
| **maximumIntrinsicEnergy** | **0.826574** | **0.826574** |
| familyEffectiveDiversity | 91.678812 | 91.341429 |
| neighborhoodEffectiveDiversity | 43.909533 | 43.495753 |

21,304 additional molecules moved the ceiling by zero at six decimals, and both
structural-diversity measures moved *down*. The ceiling is a property of the
graph, not of the sampling.

### 1.3 Why the ceiling is fixed

Eligibility already requires `valenceSatisfaction === 1`. Only two relations
survive into the shortlist at all — `satisfies` (strength 1.0) and `carries`
(0.90) — so the per-molecule mean `linkStrength` is pinned in [0.95, 1.00]. On
the shortlist:

```
0.25 · valenceSatisfaction  → 0.250          constant (it is an eligibility filter)
0.40 · linkStrength         → 0.380 … 0.400  span 0.020
0.20 · grounding            → 0.088 … 0.124  span 0.036   (bank property, not search)
0.15 · novelty              → 0.043 … 0.075  span 0.032
```

Usable dynamic range ≈ 0.088 of 1.0. Observed top-256 energy span: 0.806307 →
0.826574. `nucleusScoreFloor` is 0.765 against an observed max `finalScore` of
0.7725 — the nucleus gate sits inside the noise band of a flat landscape.

**Conclusion: the missing ingredient is not pressure on the search. It is
pressure on the vocabulary.**

## 2. Core concept

A **quark** is a derived bridge rule — `{from: port, to: port, relation,
strength}` — manufactured by the chamber rather than authored by a human. There
are 20 authored bridges in the full bank; they are the scarcest resource in the
machine.

This inserts a level into the pipeline:

```
today:  atoms ─────────────────────────────→ molecules   (rules exogenous, frozen)
v1:     atoms ──→ quarks (port-level rules) ──→ molecules (rules endogenous)
```

and it is what makes the cyclotron a **reactor** rather than a sampler. Today
100,000 independent trials run over a frozen graph with no feedback anywhere.
Quark production is the feedback path: pass *N* emits new licensing, which
changes the graph, which makes structures reachable on pass *N+1* that were
unrepresentable on pass *N*.

### 2.1 The slingshot

Bonds are generated by gravity assist over the licensed graph. Atom A offers
port `o`; waypoint C seeks `s` and offers `o'`; atom B seeks `s'`. If A—C and
C—B are both licensed, A has borrowed C's connectivity to reach B. The candidate
quark asks whether that borrowing **generalizes**: does `o → s'` license
directly, without C?

Measured on the full bank (56 atoms, 20 bridges, 191 licensed directed edges):

```
              candidate rules   confined (≥2 witnesses)
depth 1            169                  15
depth 2            404                 132
depth 3            704                 390
```

Sublinear in rules. The typed sparsity prevents collapse into transitive
closure, which would license all pairs and make novelty meaningless by
construction.

Note that confined counts rise steeply with depth partly for mechanical reasons
— more paths means more chances at ≥2 witnesses — so depth > 1 requires its own
configuration null before those counts mean anything. See §9.

## 3. Two metrics that died, and why they are recorded here

Both were measured and discarded before design. They are documented so nobody
re-proposes them.

### 3.1 Endpoint-pair generality — REFUTED

"How many new atom pairs does this rule unlock" ranks quarks by hub proximity:

```
generality EXACTLY equals offerers(o) × seekers(s):  83/89
Pearson r(generality, degree-product) = 0.987326
```

`candidate-frontier → experiment-receipt` unlocking 15 pairs is 5 offerers × 3
seekers — arithmetic on the busiest port in the graph, not a discovery.

This metric also violates **F10** of the Pressure Field Governor PDR ("no
pressure value may be authored by the candidate it ranks, or by the module that
proposed it"): generality is authored by the graph the ranker is building. The
repo's own doctrine rejects it independently of the measurement.

### 3.2 `shuffleOffersSeeks` is the wrong null for this question

`codebase-nuclei-bank.js:shuffleOffersSeeks` moves each atom's `(offers, seeks)`
bundle **as a unit** to another atom. Licensing depends only on port names, so
the port-level graph is untouched and the atom-level graph is merely relabeled —
an isomorphism. Every structural statistic is invariant under it by
construction; verified identical across 5 seeds on edges, candidate rules,
waypoint multiplicity, and hub ranking.

This is **not a defect**. For its original purpose in the nuclei ablation —
ports move while `label`, `domain`, `evidence`, and `grounding` stay put — it
genuinely breaks the atom↔port correspondence and is a valid control. It is
simply inert for topology, and topology is what quarks are made of.

## 4. Confinement, and its null

Borrowed from QCD: a quark that appears only once is an anecdote. **A candidate
rule is emitted only when ≥2 independent waypoints produce it.**

```
ritual bank (44 atoms,  8 bridges):  multiplicity {1:88, 2:1}     confined =  1
full bank   (56 atoms, 20 bridges):  multiplicity {1:154,2:14,3:1} confined = 15
```

The ritual bank cannot support the law; the full bank can.

### 4.1 Degree-matched configuration null

Preserves per-atom offer/seek counts and per-port global offer/seek frequencies;
randomizes the assignment. 200 shuffles, full bank:

```
                 null mean ± sd      real    z       p(null ≥ real)
edges            186.8 ± 4.1         191     +1.01   0.210
rules            184.5 ± 9.3         169     −1.66   0.965
CONFINED (≥2)      8.1 ± 3.2          15     +2.15   0.030
maxWaypoints       2.4 ± 0.6           3     +1.12   0.345
```

Confinement is **not** a degree artifact — degree is held fixed and the effect
survives. The signature is *concentration, not yield*: the real bank emits fewer
distinct candidates than chance (z = −1.66) but nearly twice as many
independently witnessed.

**Strength of evidence, stated plainly:** p = 0.030 uncorrected, ≈ 0.12 under
Bonferroni over the four statistics run, n = 1 bank. The ≥2 threshold was
specified before the null was run, but informally. **This licenses the
pre-registration in §8. It is not itself a result, and must not be cited as
one.** In particular, the `rules` statistic runs the other way: any claim that
the slingshot "finds many new rules" is contradicted by this table.

## 5. The relation algebra

17 relation types are present in the full bank, but only **47 compositions
actually occur**, and `satisfies` acts as an identity element. Compositions with
`satisfies` on neither side number **15** — the entire hand-authored table.

```
96 of 169 (57%)  satisfies∘satisfies
 15 compositions with satisfies on neither side
```

### 5.1 `satisfies∘satisfies → relays` (the decision)

Treating `satisfies` as a true identity would license, from three witnesses,
`atom-inventory ≡ candidate-frontier` — a sealed atom bank equated with a
retrieval result set. That equivalence is false, and it would place the
chamber's largest output class (57%) on its weakest reasoning.

Instead, composition emits a **new relation `relays`**, strictly weaker than
`satisfies`, marking *reachable-via-waypoint* rather than *directly
equivalent*, with

```
strength(quark) = λ · Π strength(composed edges),     λ < 1
```

`relays∘relays` decays further, so long chains die without a special rule.

**This is where strain lives.** Strain is not a new currency added to
`finalScore`; it is decay of `strength`, which already flows into energy through
the existing `0.40 · linkStrength` term. A relayed bond scores lower than a
direct bond *because it is physically weaker*. This preserves the discipline
established when osmosis was removed from `finalScore` on 2026-08-12: no new
scoring term, and the units stay honest
(`project-semantic-calculus-adjudicates-scores`).

### 5.2 Deriving λ

λ is bracketed by two measured constraints, not chosen:

- **Upper bound.** Authored bridge strengths in the full bank span 0.78–0.94. A
  machine-derived relay must land strictly below the minimum authored strength,
  or a derived bond could outrank every human-authored one. For the
  `satisfies∘satisfies` case (Π = 1.0) this gives **λ < 0.78**.
- **Lower bound.** A molecule containing one relay bond must still clear the
  shuffled-control bar (0.201721 on the ritual bank; to be re-measured on the
  full bank).

λ is selected by sweeping the bracket and choosing the value where
relay-bearing molecules are separable from the shuffled control **and** do not
displace direct-bond molecules at the top of the shortlist. If no λ satisfies
both, the design fails at this task and stops.

## 6. Architecture

### Layer 0 — Substrate
`buildDefaultBank(ATOM_BLUEPRINTS, BRIDGE_RULES, {})`: 56 atoms, 56 ports, 20
authored bridges, 191 licensed directed edges.

### Layer 1 — Slingshot generator (pure, structural)
Depth-*k* gravity assist emitting candidate port rules with witness sets.

**Confinement is a candidacy law, not a pressure value.** F10 forbids the
proposer from scoring its own output, so "≥2 independent witnesses" gates
*emission* and never enters the ranking. Output: 15 candidates at depth 1.

Interface: `generateQuarkCandidates(bank, {depth}) → QuarkCandidate[]`, where
each candidate carries `{from, to, witnesses[], compositions[]}` and no score.

### Layer 2 — Semantic calculus adjudication
Each candidate is a `Modulator` over `permission.ts:ModulatableState`. Pressure
sources, each naming an external producer per F10:

| Source | Tier | Behavior | Producer |
|---|---|---|---|
| `composition` | 0 | **absorbing** — no declared composition ⇒ no quark | authored relation registry |
| `contradiction` | 0 | **absorbing** — `corpusPMI` REPULSION / false-friend | `grounding-index` |
| `affinity` | 2 | ranks only | `semantotopography` port-name cosine |

Tier 0 is an absorbing zero modelled on `permission.ts:isZero` — the candidate
leaves the set, it is not ranked last (PDR F2).

`affinity` is a weak prior and is documented as such. Authored bridge rules sit
at background cosine percentiles `36.8, 53.5, 68.7, 71.1, 73.0, 74.4, 95.1,
99.0` — mean 0.71 against a null of 0.50, z ≈ 2.10, p ≈ 0.018 at n=8. There is
**no upper cutoff**: the highest-cosine authored pair is `ranked-frontier →
candidate-frontier` at 0.728 (p99), and it is correct precisely because those
ports are near-synonyms. Cosine is a floor with no ceiling and must never gate.

### Layer 3 — Grant proposal
A surviving quark widens the licensed graph, so under **F2a** it *is* a
permission grant. `applyModulation` throws `SEMANTIC_CALCULUS_PERMISSION_WIDENED`
if the chamber attempts to grant its own. The chamber emits a
`PermissionGrantRecord` proposal; a human commits it into `BRIDGE_RULES`.

This is the same shape as F9 calibration: the machine proposes, a human commits.
The chamber is structurally incapable of opening its own gate.

### Layer 4 — The orbit closes
Committed bridges re-license the graph, and the next cyclotron pass builds
molecules that were unrepresentable on the previous one.

## 7. What v1 does not do

**v1 fits no weights.** PDR F9 sets `MIN_RESOLVED = 40` and requires the
calibrator to "refuse to emit a proposal below it and report the shortfall
rather than fitting noise." The chamber has 20 authored bridges and 15 confined
candidates — below the repo's own floor.

Therefore, in v1:
- Tier 0 is a **declared law** (the 15-cell relation table), not a fit.
- Tier 2 ordering is a **declared precedence**, not a calibrated weighting.
- The calibrator stays dark until 40 grants have been resolved through F8a.

Also out of scope for v1: depth > 1 slingshots; the ω-sweep / persistent
orbiting population; any change to molecule scoring beyond the `strength` value
that `relays` already carries.

## 8. Pre-registered falsifiers

Written before implementation. Each can kill the design.

1. **Configuration null on confinement.** Re-run §4.1 with the ≥2 threshold and
   the statistic list declared in advance, Bonferroni-corrected. Fails if
   confinement does not exceed the degree-matched null.

2. **Authored-bridge recovery.** Hold out all 20 authored bridges. Does
   generator + algebra rediscover them? n = 20 on the full bank. This is the
   test most likely to kill the design outright, and it is the reason the full
   bank rather than the ritual bank is the substrate.

3. **Grant outcome predicate (F8a).** Every committed quark must be markable
   `grant_was_wrong`. A quark nobody can mark wrong is decorative prose
   (`project-checks-that-cannot-fail`).

4. **Novelty is not self-fulfilling.** Novelty is `1 − max similarity to
   constituent atoms`, so admitting *any* bond between semantically distant
   ports raises it by construction. The control must therefore be a
   **permuted-relation-algebra** run: same count of admitted quarks, same λ,
   composition table shuffled across cells. If real and permuted algebras
   produce the same novelty and verdict distributions, the algebra carries no
   information and the chamber is a noise injector.

## 9. Open questions

- **Depth.** v1 is depth 1 (15 confined candidates). On the full bank, depth 2
  yields 132 confined and depth 3 yields 390 — both clearing the F9 floor of 40,
  depth 2 by more than 3×. Neither has been run against the configuration null,
  and part of the rise is mechanical (more paths, more chances at ≥2), so the
  counts are not yet evidence. Deferred until v1's falsifiers pass; depth 2 is
  the obvious v2 candidate because it reaches the calibration floor without
  depth 3's combinatorial cost.
- **Bank growth.** If depth 2 does not survive its null, reaching 40 resolved
  grants requires more atoms. Not resolved here.
- **`relays` in `composeVector`.** `semantic-valence-cyclotron.js:composeVector`
  builds a relation vector per `offer|relation|seek`. `relays` will generate new
  keys; whether relayed bonds should contribute a distinct relation vector or
  reuse the composed edges' vectors is unresolved and affects novelty
  measurement directly.
