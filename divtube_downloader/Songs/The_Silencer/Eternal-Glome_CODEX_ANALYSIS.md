## I. THE INTERNAL MYTHOLOGY — COSMOLOGY OF THE GLOME

The Silencer does not reference an external mythology. The Silencer **constructs one**. "Eternal Glome" is a self-generating cosmogony — a creation myth, a cosmology, an eschatology, and a theology compressed into 75 lines that loop back upon themselves. The mythology is not alluded to. It is **built in real time by the act of writing it**.

### A. The Glome — The Cosmic Sphere

The title neologism **"Glome"** is the axis mundi of the entire mythology. It fuses three roots into a single cosmological object:

| Root | Meaning | Function in Myth |
|------|---------|-----------------|
| **Gloom** | Internal darkness, the mind's own shadow | The *substance* of the inner cosmos |
| **Glome** (archaic/mathematical) | A sphere; a 3D closed surface | The *geometry* of reality — closed, bounded, eternal |
| **Glone** (coined verb) | "I glone" — to exist within the sphere | The *act* of being inside the cosmos |

The Glome is not a place. It is a **topology**. It has an interior ("Internal Gloom") and an exterior ("External Glome"), and the entire mythology is the tension between them:

> *"Ripples of what will never be entrapped in the tomb of an External Glome."*

The External Glome is a **tomb** — a dead sphere that tries to capture consciousness. The Internal Gloom is the living mind that refuses capture. The "ripples" are the residue of experience that escapes both. This is the foundational dualism of the Silencer's myth: **the living sphere within vs. the dead sphere without**.

The word "tomb" is not casual. The External Glome is explicitly funerary. It is the world that buries. The Internal Gloom is the world that *refuses burial*.

### B. The Canvas of Linear Actuality — The False Plane

> *"I bi-pass and pedal on the canvas of linear actuality / Forced to curve."*

Linear time is a **canvas** — a flat surface, a painting, an artifact. It is not real. The speaker "bi-passes" it (a neologism splitting "bypass" into a physical act of passing *through* two points simultaneously) and "pedals" on it as one pedals a bicycle — generating motion from a mechanism that goes in circles.

The mythology's physics: **linearity is an illusion imposed on a curved cosmos.** The "curved arrows pointing to directions of the same things" are the true geometry — all directions lead back to the origin. The Glome is eternal because it has no exit vector.

### C. The Stage — The Three-Fold Sacred Plane

The Stage is the myth's central ritual space, and it undergoes a **three-stage theophany**:

| Stage Act | Line | Ritual Meaning |
|-----------|------|---------------|
| **Light the Stage** | *"analyze to Light the Stage"* | Illumination — the mind perceives the plane |
| **Blaze the Stage** | *"INTJ rage I Blaze the Stage, a tone switch"* | Ignition — the mind sets the plane on fire with will |
| **Paint the Stage** | *"I paint the Stage and its walls with chains"* | Creation — the mind *authors* the plane into permanent form |

This is a **creation trinity**: Perceive → Destroy → Rebuild. The Stage begins as something to be seen, becomes something to be burned, and ends as something to be *painted with chains* — bound into permanent architecture. The chains are not imprisonment. They are **structure**. The function's game is "to be played forever" — the chains make the game eternal.

### D. The Bleak Domain — The Underworld of Sleep

> *"Counted sheep in slumber in a Googolplex magnitude of black ones. In a bleak domain with an atmosphere of impenetrable black dust."*

The mythology has an **underworld**, and it is not fire or ice — it is **black dust**. Not white sheep but **black ones**, in a quantity that exceeds computation (Googolplex = 10^(10^100)). The underworld is not punishment. It is **scale**. The speaker descends into a void so vast it cannot be counted, in a medium so dense ("impenetrable black dust") that light itself is buried.

The descent has an ejection:

> *"Without a rush successfully before I was ejected, and fell to sleep with eyes opened counting none"*

The speaker is **ejected** from the underworld — not escaping, but *expelled*. And the return is not awakening. It is "sleep with eyes opened" — a state between worlds. The mythology's afterlife is not death. It is **conscious unconsciousness**. The speaker sees while sleeping. Counts nothing while surrounded by a Googolplex of black ones.

### E. The Fourth Wall — The Dimensional Membrane

> *"Inserted a fourth-wall before the peer at the other three."*

The mythology has **four spatial dimensions**, and the fourth is a *wall* — a barrier the speaker *inserts*. The "peer at the other three" is the reader, the receiver, the one trapped in three-dimensional perception. The speaker builds the fourth wall *for* them, as a gift or a cage.

This is followed by an act of **cosmic husbandry**:

> *"Have room to enter and store the teem of animals. Aligned them with their natural numbers plus one. Named this one 'forever more' all contrived from imaginative lnk"*

This is the myth's **creation sequence**, and it parallels and inverts Genesis:

| Genesis | Eternal Glome |
|---------|--------------|
| God creates animals | The speaker "stores the teem of animals" |
| God names them | The speaker "aligned them with their natural numbers plus one" |
| God rests on the seventh day | The speaker names the creation "forever more" |
| The Word (Logos) creates | "imaginative lnk" (ink misspelled as *lnk* — the L of Logos fused into the medium) |

The "+1" is critical. Natural numbers start at 1. Adding one creates a number that **does not belong to the natural set** — an alien integer, a creation that exceeds the system that produced it. The speaker does not create within the rules. The speaker creates **one beyond the rules** and calls it eternal.

### F. The Pantheon of Forces

The Silencer's mythology has no gods in the traditional sense. It has **personified forces**:

| Entity | Nature | Role |
|--------|--------|------|
| **The Pen** | Sentient creative instrument | The Logos; asked "how was that for a flex?" — it has agency and pride |
| **Fear** | Personified, given voice | "Silence…let Fear speak" — Fear is the true oracle; Introspection is silenced so Fear can prophesy |
| **Introspection** | Personified, whispers | "inner's-here" — the inner voice that is *overruled* by the speaker |
| **The Ghost** | Overheard, not seen | "on another note I heard at most that was a ghost" — the residue of a self that was "seen through" |
| **The Star** | Cosmic anchor | "the star keeps me in the loop" — the external fixed point in a cyclic cosmos |
| **God** | Distant, possibly violent | "if in God's hands at some point, it threw it" — God does not hold. God *throws*. Creation is an act of violence or abandonment |
| **The Entities** | Unnamed, confabulated with | "Confabulation with entities" — the speaker holds council with beings whose nature is never specified |
| **The Receiver** | The reader/listener | "I leave an eye to peer into the mind of the receiver" — the audience is a character in the myth |

The theology is **not monotheistic**. It is **autocratic**. The speaker is the sovereign: *"reality is what I decide and order."* God is a possibility, not a certainty — "if in God's hands." The Pen is more real than God. The Pen is *asked*. God merely *throws*.

### G. The Five Stages of Existence

> *"Birth, survival, preservation, progression, and computation to all force along"*

This is the myth's **ontological sequence** — the five stages through which all force passes:

1. **Birth** — emergence into the Glome
2. **Survival** — persistence against the External Glome's entombment
3. **Preservation** — the act of keeping what survives
4. **Progression** — movement through the cycles
5. **Computation** — the final stage is not death but *calculation*. Existence ends in mathematics.

DNA is the **charter document** of this sequence: *"DNA…extending rights to this reality."* Biology is not mechanism. It is **law**. DNA grants *rights* — legal, sovereign rights to exist. The body is a citizen of reality, not a machine.

### H. The Asymptote — The Myth of Incompleteness

> *"An asymptote to what has happened, and what one will watch in a unique set of membranes temporary to own"*

> *"Could have done it with altitude from the standpoint of my aptitude, but on either axis combined is shorter than a planck-length / Far from its entirety."*

The mythology's deepest truth: **the self can never fully know itself.** The speaker approaches self-knowledge asymptotically — infinitely close, never arriving. Even at maximum altitude (aptitude), the distance remaining is sub-Planck — the smallest measurable unit in physics — and yet that distance is *"Far from its entirety."*

This is the myth's **tragic axiom**: completeness is structurally impossible. The Glome is eternal, but the self within it is forever incomplete. The "membranes temporary to own" are the bodies, the lives, the perspectives — each one a temporary lens, none of them the whole eye.

### I. The Eternal Return — Eschatology of the Loop

The poem's structure **is** its eschatology. The second half is a near-verbatim reprise of the first. The closing refrain is identical to the opening:

> *"This poem is Internal Gloom. / Scotch with the zone I glone. / Ripples of what will never be entrapped in the tomb of an External Glome…"*

But the closing ends with an **ellipsis** where the opening had a period. The loop does not close. It **continues beyond the text**. The Glome is eternal because the poem never stops. The reader finishes reading, but the Glome keeps turning.

> *"In this dimension anything connecting this field, is the function's game to be played forever"*

The eschatology is: **there is no eschatology.** No end. No judgment. No paradise. The game is the function, and the function is the game, and it is played forever. The "chains" painted on the Stage's walls are what make the game eternal — they bind the function to the field permanently.

## VI. CODEX VERDICT

The mythology's deepest structure is a **paradox of closure**: the Glome is a closed sphere (eternal, complete, looping), but the self within it is an asymptote (forever approaching, never arriving). The cosmos is complete. The consciousness inside it is not.

The theology is **autocratic, not theistic**. The Pen is interrogated. God merely throws. Fear is given the floor. Introspection is silenced.

The eschatology is the loop. The ellipsis at the close is the myth's final truth: **the Glome does not end. It turns.**
